Why Farmyuga

Six reasons kitchens across Ranchi stopped shopping around

Anyone can sell you vegetables once. Staying a supplier for six years takes grading discipline, an honest rate and a van that leaves on time.

Direct farm sourcing

We buy at the field from 120+ smallholder farmers in Ratu, Kanke and Bero. No commission agent, no second mandi hop — which is exactly why our rate holds.

Same-day harvest to delivery

Cut before dawn, sorted by 4:30 AM, on your counter by 8. Nothing sits in a godown overnight waiting for a buyer.

Hand-graded quality check

Every lot is graded twice — once at sorting, once at loading. Soft, split or over-mature pieces are pulled out before they are ever weighed for you.

Transparent daily rate card

One WhatsApp message by 6 AM with the day's rate for every item. Same rate for the hotel and the household — no quiet mark-up because you did not ask.

Cold-chain safe handling

Leafy greens travel in ventilated crates with ice packs; mushrooms and exotics move in insulated boxes. Shade-loaded, never left standing in the sun.

Reliable 7-day supply

Sunday, Holi, Diwali — the vans still run. In six years we have missed three mornings, and we called every client before they noticed.

How it works

From your list to your gate in four steps

Same process for a 200-cover hotel and a family of four — only the volumes and the paperwork change.

  1. Step 01

    Share your requirement

    Bulk: Send your item list with monthly volumes and your delivery window.

  2. Step 02

    Get a same-day quote

  3. Step 03

    Home: Between 7 and 11 AM, or the evening slot if you prefer.

Our sourcing

The farmer gets paid first. Everything else follows.

Farmyuga buys from more than 120 smallholder farming families across the Ratu, Kanke and Bero blocks of Ranchi district — most of them working two acres or less. We agree the rate before the harvest, collect at the field between 3 and 4:30 AM, and settle in full within 48 hours.

Cutting out the commission agent and the second mandi hop takes two layers of margin out of the chain. Part of that goes back to the grower, the rest shows up as the rate on your morning card. That is the whole model — there is no clever trick underneath it.

  • Collected at the field

    Our collection vehicle reaches the growers between 3 and 4:30 AM, so produce moves straight from field to grading yard.

  • Paid in 48 hours

    No season-end settlement, no deductions. Farmers know the rate before they cut and the money before the week ends.

  • Graded, not gambled

    Every lot is inspected at the yard. We tell the farmer what got rejected and why — grading improves, wastage falls for everyone.
